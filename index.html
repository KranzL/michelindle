How to play

Every day the kitchen serves three courses: three real restaurants holding two or three Michelin stars. Each course is a photograph of a plate. Your job is to name the signature dish on it.

prix fixe

You are shown the restaurant and the photograph. Choose the signature dish from four options. Three guesses.

à la carte

Only the photograph is served. Search the full carte and call both the dish and the house in six guesses. Misses reveal the house's cuisine, country, and city along the way, and tell you whether you matched the country, the course, and the stars.

Your mode is locked once the first course begins. A new service starts at midnight EST.
